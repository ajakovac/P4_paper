READER-FACING OUTCOMES

NO SOLUTION FOUND - local KS-based solution not found.
SOLUTION FOUND BUT REJECTED - no admissible KS-based derivation.
SOLUTION FOUND - used [represented rules/procedures/composition].

Technical failures are kept separate from scientific outcomes:
EXECUTION ERROR - proposer response not obtained.

READER_DIAGNOSIS.txt contains only the compact human-readable result.
E2_READER_SUMMARY.txt / E3_READER_SUMMARY.txt collect those lines for a full experiment.
Detailed diagnostic codes and stderr/stdout remain in the audit/check bundles.
